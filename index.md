## `tessl8`

`tessl8` LLC exists to help computing system educators to create scalable, hands-on activities to help students learn how systems work while relying as much as possible on inexpensive platforms and open-source tools to create replicable infrastructure that integrates with learning management systems.

### Portfolio

We're currently working on creating a portfolio of publicly available course materials to highlight what we've built in the past.  This portfolio represents only a small sample, as many of our designs are maintained privately by our customers to restrict access and protect students and their work.

### Working with `tessl8`

`tessl8` acts as a curriculum development consultancy and maintains no licensing rights to any materials we develop for our customers, other than offering our existing open-source components as parts of a design.  In some cases, we can offer additional system administration services to help manage a deployed design or infrastructure on an individual basis.  Please contact us to discuss how we can work together.
